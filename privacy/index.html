1. Who we are

Ninnim ("Ninnim", "the app") is developed and published by Mehmet Emre Karamustafa, an individual developer based in Türkiye ("we", "us", "our"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact: mehmetemrekaramustafa@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • No account. Ninnim has no sign-up and no login.
  • What the microphone hears stays on your device. Ninnim uses the microphone only when you choose to: while you record your own voice (for example shushing, humming or a lullaby) for your baby, and, if you turn on the optional "Play again if baby wakes" feature, to listen for crying after the sleep timer ends. Recordings are processed and stored on your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Listening is processed on your device in real time; nothing is recorded, stored or sent. Ninnim does not use the camera and does not request access to your location, contacts or photos.
  • Your baby profile stays on your device. The optional nickname and age range, as well as your settings, favorites, mixes and routines, are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • A few service providers. We use Google AdMob for banner ads in the free version (with Google's consent tool in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Apple or Google and RevenueCat for subscriptions, Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports and Mixpanel for usage statistics.
  • Usage statistics are your choice. If you allow them, the app sends Mixpanel which features you use, with a random identifier and no name, recordings or location. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland they are off until you turn them on; elsewhere they are on and you can turn them off at any time in Settings › Privacy.
  • No tracking prompt on iOS. We do not ask for permission to track you and we do not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) on iPhone or iPad.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

4. Data processed by our service providers

4.1 Advertising (free version only): Google AdMob

  • When: The free version shows a single standard banner ad at the bottom of some screens (Home, Explore, categories, Library, Routines, Settings and the player), at any time of day. There are no ads on the sleep screen, in nursery mode, in the mixer and timer panels, during onboarding, on the paywall or while you record your voice. We do not use full-screen, rewarded or app-open ads. No ad requests are made while the app is in the background or the screen is off. Premium subscribers see no ads and no ad requests are made for them.
  • Provider: Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited (Google AdMob).
  • Data: When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information such as your IP address (used to estimate your approximate location, e.g. country or city), device and operating-system information, app information, ad interactions (impressions, taps), diagnostic and performance data and device identifiers. On Android, this can include the Android advertising ID and app set ID, subject to your device settings. On iOS, we do not request tracking permission, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the SDK; the SDK may use identifiers that are limited to our app or to us as developer (such as Apple's identifier for vendor) for purposes like frequency capping and fraud prevention.
  • Purposes: to serve and measure ads, cap how often an ad is shown, prevent fraud and abuse and, only where you allow it, personalize ads.
  • Your choices:
    • EEA, UK and Switzerland: we use Google's User Messaging Platform (a Google-certified consent management platform using the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) to ask for your consent. If you do not consent, Google serves non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy › Ad preferences (shown where consent is required).
    • Other regions: you can limit ad personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads).
    • iPhone and iPad: we ask Google to show only non-personalized ads.
    • Premium removes ads completely.
  • Content: Ninnim is intended for adults. We ask Google to show only family-friendly ads (maximum ad content rating "G").
  • More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4.2 Subscriptions: Apple App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat

  • Payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment card details, your name or your e-mail address from the store.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) manages subscriptions for us as our service provider. When you open the app, buy, or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives a random app user ID that the app creates on your device (the same code as your support ID in Settings › Support; it is not linked to your name or e-mail address), the store's purchase receipt or token, your purchase history for Ninnim (product, purchase and expiry dates, trial and renewal status, store, country and currency, price), and technical information such as device model, operating-system version, app version and IP address.
  • Purposes: to verify purchases, unlock Premium, restore purchases on your other devices, handle billing issues and grace periods, prevent fraud, and show us aggregate subscription statistics.
  • More information: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4.3 Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics

  • Provider: Google LLC (Firebase).
  • Data: if the app crashes or hits a serious error, a crash report is sent. It contains the stack trace (which part of the code failed), the device model, operating-system and app versions, device state at the time of the crash (such as free memory and orientation) and a random installation identifier. Crash reports do not include your baby profile, your mixes or any content you create.
  • Purpose: to find and fix bugs and keep playback reliable through the night.
  • Retention: crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Your choice: you can turn crash reporting off in Settings › Privacy › Send crash reports.

4.4 Usage statistics: Mixpanel

  • Provider: Mixpanel, Inc. (USA), which processes this data for us as our service provider (processor). Our Mixpanel project is hosted in Mixpanel's EU data centre.
  • When: only if Settings › Privacy › Usage statistics is on. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (based on your device's region setting) this switch is off until you turn it on. In other regions it is on by default and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Data:
    • events from the app, such as: the app was opened, onboarding was completed, a sound, routine, the sleep screen, nursery mode, "Crying? Soothe now" or the night light was started, a night session or a routine was completed, a night session was interrupted, a timer was set, a mix or a recording was saved (type and length only), lyrics or a shared link were opened, something was shared or imported, the subscription screen was shown, a trial was started, a purchase was completed or restored, an ad was shown, "Play again if baby wakes" was turned on or played again, and what you chose on the morning summary. Events carry only small details: sound IDs, the name of the screen or source, counts and durations. Routines you create are recorded only as "custom";
    • technical information that Mixpanel's software adds to each event: operating system and version, device model, app version, screen size, language, mobile carrier and whether you are on Wi-Fi;
    • a random identifier created for this installation of the app.
  • What is not sent: your name, your baby's nickname, your e-mail address, your recordings or any audio, your support ID, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA or Android advertising ID) or your location. We have turned off Mixpanel's IP-based location: Mixpanel receives your IP address when the app connects to it, as any internet service does, but does not use it to determine or store a location. We do not use Mixpanel's automatic event collection.
  • Purpose: to understand, in aggregate, which features are used, whether night-time playback runs without interruption and how the subscription screen and free trial perform, so that we can improve the app. We do not use this data for advertising, we do not combine it with data from other apps or websites to track you, we do not link it to your name or other identity, and we do not sell it.
  • "Anonymous": the statistics are not linked to your name or contact details. Because they use a random identifier, data protection law may still treat them as personal data, and we handle them as such.
  • Legal basis: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, your consent (you turn the switch on). Elsewhere, our legitimate interest in improving the app, with the option to turn statistics off at any time.
  • Retention: event data is kept for up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Your choice: turn Settings › Privacy › Usage statistics off at any time; from then on no statistics are sent. Settings › Privacy › Delete all my data also replaces the random identifier, so later statistics are not connected to earlier ones.
  • More information: Mixpanel Privacy Policy.

4.5 Support e-mails

If you contact us by e-mail, we use your e-mail address and the content of your message to answer you. We keep this correspondence for up to 24 months after our last exchange, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

4.6 Our website

Our website (ninnim.app), where this policy is published, is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). The host may log your IP address and browser information for security. We do not use cookies or analytics on the website.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or profiles of you.
  • We never record or listen without your choice, and we never receive any audio. The microphone is used only while you record your voice and, if you turn it on, while "Play again if baby wakes" listens for crying after the sleep timer. Your recordings stay on your device: we do not receive, upload or share them. Listening is processed on your device in real time: nothing is recorded, stored or sent, and no audio is given to our service providers.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos or health data.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your data for any purpose that is not described in this policy.
  • We do not use usage statistics for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Under some US state laws, allowing an ad network to use device identifiers for personalized ads may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out at any time with the device and in-app controls in section 4.1.

7. Children

Ninnim is a tool for parents and caregivers aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children and children should not use it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, including children under 13 in the United States. The optional baby profile describes your baby, but it is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or to our service providers. If you turn on "Play again if baby wakes", the microphone may hear your baby, but the sound is only analysed as volume levels on your device in real time; nothing is recorded or sent. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by e-mail), please contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Our service providers (Google, RevenueCat and Mixpanel) process data in the United States and other countries. Usage statistics are stored in Mixpanel's EU data centre; Mixpanel, Inc. is a US company and may access them from outside the EEA, for example for support. Where personal data from the EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred to countries without an adequacy decision, the providers rely on safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (where they are certified) and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. For users in Türkiye, transfers abroad are made under Article 9 of Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK), using the appropriate safeguards it provides.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct it;
  • have it deleted;
  • restrict or object to processing (including processing based on legitimate interests);
  • data portability;
  • withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing before withdrawal);
  • complain to us first, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (for example your local EU authority, the UK Information Commission, formerly the Information Commissioner's Office, or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

Users in Türkiye have the rights listed in Article 11 of the KVKK (see the Turkish version and the KVKK Information Notice).

How to exercise your rights: e-mail us at mehmetemrekaramustafa@gmail.com. Because Ninnim has no accounts, we may need an identifier to find your data: for subscription data, copy the support ID in Settings › Support › Copy support ID and include it in your e-mail. We answer within one month (30 days under the KVKK). For advertising data, you can also use Google's tools and your device's ad settings. Usage statistics use a random identifier that is not linked to your support ID or to you, so we usually cannot find the statistics of a particular person; you can stop them at any time with the switch in section 4.4, and they are deleted at the end of the retention period.

11. Your controls in the app and on your device

  • Ad preferences: Settings › Privacy › Ad preferences (shown where consent is required, e.g. in the EEA, UK and Switzerland).
  • Crash reports: Settings › Privacy › Send crash reports.
  • Usage statistics: Settings › Privacy › Usage statistics (off by default in the EEA, UK and Switzerland; on by default elsewhere).
  • Notifications: Settings › Notifications › Bedtime reminder (off by default), and your device's notification settings.
  • Advertising ID (Android): your device's Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Remove ads: Ninnim Premium.
  • Microphone: asked for only when you tap record in "Your voice" or turn on "Play again if baby wakes". You can turn it off at any time in your device's settings; recordings you already made keep playing, and listening stops. Delete a recording in Your voice › ⋯ › Delete recording.
  • Play again if baby wakes (Ninnim Premium): off by default. Turn it on or off in the sleep timer, in nursery mode or in Settings › Play again if baby wakes, where you can also choose the sensitivity, how long the sound plays again and when listening stops. While Ninnim listens, your device shows its microphone indicator (on Android also a "Ninnim is listening" notification). Listening ends at the latest at the morning time you chose (for a daytime nap, after at most 4 hours).
  • Delete all on-device data: Settings › Privacy › Delete all my data (this also deletes your voice recordings and the play log for the morning summary), or delete the app. Deleting gives the app a new support ID and a new usage-statistics identifier, and sets the usage-statistics switch back to its default, so copy the old support ID first if you also want us to delete data held by our service providers (section 10).

12. Security

Data that our service providers send over the internet is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Data on your device is protected by your operating system's app sandbox. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit the data we process to what is described here.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
