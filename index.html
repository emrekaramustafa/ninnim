Baby sleep sounds, made for parents

One tap to a peaceful night.

Ninnim is a calm sleep sounds app made for parents. Start white noise, a soft shush or an instrumental lullaby with a single tap, dim the screen, and let it play through the night while your little one sleeps.

  • Download on the App Store
  • Get it on Google Play

For iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets. Free to use, with an optional Ninnim Premium subscription.

Made for the middle of the night

  • Designed for 2 a.m.

    A dark, night-friendly look, “Start last night’s sound” in one tap, and sounds that fade in gently and never jump to full volume.

  • Works offline

    Every sound is stored on your device, so Ninnim works on a plane, in the car or at the grandparents’ house. No Wi-Fi or mobile data needed.

  • A timer that fades out

    Choose anything from 15 minutes to 12 hours, or let it play all night. When the timer ends, the sound fades out slowly instead of stopping abruptly.

  • Private by design

    No account and no sign-up. The microphone is used only when you choose: to record your own voice, and for the optional “Play again if baby wakes”, which listens on your device and records nothing. Your recordings, like your baby’s optional nickname and age range, stay on your device.

Also in Ninnim: a sound mixer, bedtime routines, and a nursery mode that turns a spare tablet into a dedicated sound device.

Free, with an optional Premium

The free version has no time limit on playback and includes 18 sounds (played one at a time), one recording of your voice, lullaby lyrics, the timer, the sleep screen, nursery mode and the first bedtime routine. A small banner ad appears on some screens, never on the sleep screen or in nursery mode, and there are no full-screen ads.

Ninnim Premium is a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. It unlocks the full library, the mixer (up to 5 sounds at once and saved mixes), all routines and your own, the morning summary, “Play again if baby wakes” (beta) and unlimited recordings, and removes ads. The price is shown in the app before you buy.

Safe listening

Keep the volume at the lowest level that works and place your device away from the crib: at least 30 cm (1 ft), and farther if you can. Ninnim is not a medical device and not a baby monitor. Talk to your pediatrician about your baby’s sleep.
