Shared from Ninnim

Someone shared a sleep mix or routine with you.

When Ninnim is installed, this link opens right in the app. You see what was shared, the sounds and their lengths, before anything is added. If you are reading this page, Ninnim is not on this phone yet or the link opened in a browser.

  1. Get Ninnim from your app store.
  2. Tap the link you were sent once more. It now opens in Ninnim.
  3. Still in the browser? Copy the link, then in Ninnim open Library › Mixes › “Open a shared link” and paste it.
  • Open Ninnim
  • Download on the App Store
  • Get it on Google Play

The link only holds sound names, volumes and lengths. Nothing is uploaded, and this page loads nothing from anywhere else.
