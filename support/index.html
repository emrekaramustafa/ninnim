Does Ninnim work offline?

Yes. Every sound is stored on your device, so Ninnim plays without Wi-Fi or mobile data: on a plane, in the car or at the grandparents’ house.

An internet connection is only used to buy or restore Ninnim Premium, to load the small banner ad in the free version and to send crash reports. Your Premium status is saved on your device, so Premium keeps working offline too.

How do the sleep timer and fade-out work?

  • In the player, open the sleep timer and choose a duration from 15 minutes to 12 hours, set a custom duration, or choose Unlimited to play until you stop it.
  • When the time is nearly up, the sound fades out slowly instead of stopping abruptly. You choose how long the fade-out takes, and the timer shows when the fade starts and when the sound stops.
  • You can change the default fade-out length in Settings › Playback › Fade-out when stopping.
  • Sounds also fade in gently when they start, so they never jump to full volume.

The sound stopped during the night. What can I do?

Ninnim is built to play all night with the screen off and your device’s default settings. Phone calls, alarms, other apps that play audio, a very low battery and some battery-saving features can still pause or stop playback. These tips help.

On every device

  • Keep the device charging or well charged. Keep the charger and cable out of reach and away from bedding.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb or a Sleep focus so that calls and notifications don’t interrupt the sound.
  • In Ninnim, turn on Settings › Playback › Resume after interruptions so playback continues after a short interruption.
  • Leave Ninnim running in the background. Swiping it away in the app switcher or the recent apps screen closes the app and stops the sound.

iPhone and iPad

  • Ninnim keeps playing when the screen is locked and when your iPhone is in silent mode.
  • Starting another app that plays sound (a video, music or a voice message) takes over the audio and pauses Ninnim.

Android: battery optimisation

Some Android phones stop background apps to save battery. If the sound stops after a while, allow Ninnim to run without battery restrictions. Menu names vary by manufacturer and Android version.

  • Most phones: Settings › Apps › Ninnim › Battery (or App battery usage) › Unrestricted.
  • Samsung: also open Settings › Battery (or Battery and device care › Battery) › Background usage limits. Ninnim should not be listed under Sleeping apps or Deep sleeping apps; you can add it to Never sleeping apps.
  • Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO: App info › Battery saver › No restrictions, and turn on Autostart.
  • Other brands: the independent site dontkillmyapp.com has step-by-step guides for each manufacturer.

How do I restore my purchase?

  1. Make sure your device is signed in with the same Apple Account (iPhone, iPad) or Google account (Android) that you used to subscribe.
  2. In Ninnim, open Settings › Support and tap Restore purchases. The same link is at the bottom of the Premium screen.

Purchases do not transfer between the App Store and Google Play: a subscription bought on an iPhone or iPad cannot be restored on an Android device, and the other way round.

Still not working? Write to us and include your support ID from Settings › Support › Copy support ID. We never need your password or payment details.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the App Store or Google Play, so you cancel them there. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. After you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the period you have paid for.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the Settings app and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then Ninnim.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription. To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period or free trial ends.

You can also go to App Store › your profile picture › Subscriptions. Apple’s guide: Apple Support: cancel a subscription.

Android

  1. Open the Google Play app and tap your profile icon.
  2. Tap Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, then Ninnim.
  3. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the steps. Cancel before the renewal date or before the free trial ends.

You can also manage subscriptions on the web at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. Google’s guide: Google Play Help: cancel a subscription.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds follow their policies. For Apple, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (Apple Support: request a refund). For Google Play, follow Google Play’s refund process. See also section 6 of our Terms of Use.

How do I use nursery mode, Guided Access or app pinning?

Nursery mode turns a spare iPad, tablet or phone into a dedicated sound device: the screen stays dark and the controls lock against accidental taps. It is free and shows no ads.

  1. Open Settings › Baby & safety › Nursery mode, or open it from the player.
  2. Follow the short set-up guide: connect the charger, turn on Do Not Disturb, and check the volume and the distance to the crib.
  3. To unlock the controls, touch and hold the screen.

To keep the device in Ninnim so that nobody can leave the app by accident, use your device’s own lock.

iPhone and iPad: Guided Access

  1. Go to Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access, turn it on and set a passcode.
  2. Open nursery mode in Ninnim and triple-click the side button (or the Home button on devices that have one) to start Guided Access.
  3. To end it, triple-click the same button again and enter your passcode.

Apple Support: use Guided Access

Android: app pinning

  1. Turn on App pinning in Settings › Security (or Security & privacy › More security settings). The location varies by manufacturer.
  2. Open Ninnim, go to the recent apps screen, tap the Ninnim icon at the top of its window and choose Pin.
  3. To unpin, touch and hold Back and Overview, or swipe up and hold, depending on your navigation settings.

Android Help: pin and unpin screens

Safe listening

  • Volume. Keep the volume at the lowest level that works. You can set a low default in Settings › Playback › Starting volume.
  • Distance. Place your device away from your baby’s sleep space: at least 30 cm (1 ft), and farther if possible. Never put it inside the crib or cot.
  • Charging. If the device is charging during the night, keep it and its cable out of reach and away from bedding, pillows and mattresses.
  • Not a medical device. Ninnim does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, including colic or sleep problems. Ask your doctor or another qualified health professional about your baby’s sleep, health and safe-sleep practices.
  • Not a baby monitor. Ninnim does not monitor your baby and cannot alert you to anything. The optional “Play again if baby wakes” only listens for crying, on your device, to start the sound again; it records nothing and does not watch over your baby. Ninnim does not replace adult supervision.
  • Playback may stop. The operating system, battery-saving features, a low battery, phone calls, other apps or updates can pause or stop playback. Do not rely on Ninnim where uninterrupted sound is critical.

How do I report an inappropriate ad?

The free version shows a small Google AdMob banner on some screens (never on the sleep screen or in nursery mode), and we ask Google to show only family-friendly ads (maximum ad content rating “G”). If you still see an ad that seems inappropriate:

  • In Ninnim, go to Settings › Support › Report an ad. This opens a pre-filled e-mail: add when you saw the ad, on which screen, and what it showed. A screenshot helps.
  • Or e-mail the same details to our support address.

We review every report and block the ad or the advertiser in AdMob. Ninnim Premium removes ads completely.

How do I delete my data?

Ninnim has no account. Your optional baby profile (nickname and age range), settings, favorites, mixes, routines, voice recordings and the play log behind the morning summary are stored only on your device. We never receive them. To delete them:

  • in Ninnim, use Settings › Privacy › Delete all my data (Ninnim then starts over with a new support ID), or
  • uninstall the app, which removes all of Ninnim’s data from the device.

If you use iCloud Backup or Google backup, your device backup may still contain a copy; Apple or Google manage those backups. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription (see how to cancel).

Our service providers process a small amount of data: subscription records (RevenueCat) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, kept for up to 90 days). To access or delete this data, e-mail mehmetemrekaramustafa@gmail.com and include your support ID from Settings › Support › Copy support ID. Copy it before you use “Delete all my data”, because the app gets a new ID afterwards. For advertising data, you can also use Google’s tools and your device’s ad settings. Details are in sections 9 and 10 of our Privacy Policy.
